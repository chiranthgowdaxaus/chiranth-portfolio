What I’m into.

Outside work, I’m happiest when I’m moving, exploring, admiring a good machine, or finding something great to eat.

01
cars + bikes

Anything on wheels

I’m into cars and bikes — especially BMWs. I love the mix of design, engineering and character that makes a machine feel special.

02
outdoors + travel

Taking the long way

Hiking, exploring new places and travelling help me reset. I like the views, the unexpected turns and the stories that come home with me.

03
running + swimming

Keeping in motion

Jogging, running and swimming clear my head. I like the rhythm, the consistency and the better energy I have afterwards.

04
food

Always up for pani puri

Pani puri is an easy yes. I love trying local food, finding small favourites and sharing something delicious with good company.

A few things I believe.

01

Curiosity beats pretending to know

The best work usually starts with a better question.

02

Clarity is a form of respect

Whether it’s a chart, an interface or a conversation, people should not have to fight the work.

03

Useful can still be beautiful

Function and feeling are not opposites. I like the point where they reinforce each other.
