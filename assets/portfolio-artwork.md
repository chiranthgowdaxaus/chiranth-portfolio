# Portfolio artwork

Production artwork for the October 2026 green-gold direction. All four raster assets were created with the built-in image generation tool, inspected, and optimized to WebP. The original portrait and tool brand logos are not AI-generated.

| Asset | Production brief |
| --- | --- |
| `glass-gold-atmosphere.webp` | 1600 × 1000 dark glass material; blurred olive and gold lighting, black negative space for copy, no lettering or UI. |
| `paidly-hero-gold.webp` | 1000 × 1200 transparent product presentation; black smartphone held in a natural hand, using the existing sample Paidly interface as the visual reference; warm ivory screen, olive-gold rim light, no unrelated metrics or brands. Presentation artwork, not a screenshot of a shipped release. |
| `sensor-silver-study.webp` | 900 × 600 illustrative silver signal traces and fine dots on charcoal; no labels, numeric values or measured-result claims. |
| `stadium-silver-study.webp` | 1774 × 887 architectural oval stadium study with dense crowd dots in silver on charcoal; illustration, not a real captured heatmap. |

The homepage bill and workout previews are native HTML, not rasterized screenshots. Their values are explicitly labelled as samples. The full case-study demos remain interactive and local-only.

UI icons: Phosphor Icons Light, sourced from the official `phosphor-icons/core` repository. License retained in `icons/phosphor/LICENSE`. Source: https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core
