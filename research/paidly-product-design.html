PaidlyBills, made clearer.

A bill tracker for iPhone. It reminds you before a bill is due and keeps reminding you until it's paid. I designed it and built it natively in SwiftUI.

My role
Product design & iOS development
Platform

When

What it does

Paidly answers three questions at a glance: what's due, when, and has it been paid? It records payments; it never moves money or connects to a bank.

Next bill first

Home leads with the next bill as one big card. Slide to mark it paid, and a repeating bill creates its next one automatically.

Reminders until it's paid

Alerts start a few days before the due date and repeat daily until the bill is marked paid, right from the notification.

The month at a glance

What's left, what's paid and income for the month, with a calendar that marks every due date.

Family splits

Share a bill with a family group, split it equally or by amount, and track who has settled up.

Widgets and Watch

Home and lock screen widgets show what's due next. A separate Apple Watch app works on its own.

Works offline

Bills stay on the phone without an account. Signing in syncs them to the web, Android and Watch apps.

How it's built

Native iPhone app

SwiftUI with no third-party packages, iOS 18 and later. Local notifications with a Mark as paid action, WidgetKit widgets, haptics and sounds that respect Reduce Motion.

One backend for every device

Supabase and PostgreSQL with row-level security, so personal bills stay private and shared bills are visible only to the family group. Revision checks stop an old edit overwriting a newer one.

Swift · SwiftUI · WidgetKit · Supabase · PostgreSQL. Notification delivery still needs testing on physical devices.
