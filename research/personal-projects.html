Paidly · 2026

Bill tracking & splitting

Track recurring bills, record payments and split shared costs equally or by custom amounts. PostgreSQL access rules keep personal records private and limit shared records to group members.

Revision checks reject stale edits. Marking a bill paid creates its next recurrence once, while keeping the payment history.

UI/UX case study: flows, navigation and payment states

TypeScript · React Native · Expo · Supabase · PostgreSQL

iOS build uploaded for internal TestFlight testing. Two-account split syncing and physical-device alerts still need testing. The app records balances; it does not transfer money.

Monday Transit · 2026

Victorian public transport

A Mac and iPhone app with nearby PTV departures, transport filters and downloaded maps covering Victoria. Reported vehicle positions and timetable estimates have separate labels.

MapLibre and Three.js use the same map camera for vehicle models. Missing position data stays missing rather than being filled with invented movement.

Swift · SwiftUI · REST APIs · MapLibre · PMTiles · Three.js

Development builds verified on Mac and iPhone simulator. Live data samples nearby services, not the whole fleet. Exact track-following and physical iPhone performance remain open.

Android test build created and iOS build uploaded for TestFlight. Phone testing, health syncing and account-link setup still need verification.
