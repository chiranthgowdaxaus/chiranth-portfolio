Technical notes & decisionsDesign surfaces, bilingual labels and scope

One identity, five surfaces

Worked across app concepts, social campaigns, brochures and store communication, packaging explorations, and retail labels. Reused the same identity and layout principles so the outputs felt connected.

The decisions that mattered

  • Design for the medium: a social thumbnail, phone screen and printed shelf strip have different contrast, scale and spacing needs.
  • Bilingual hierarchy: balanced Kannada and English through optical sizing and a clear reading order, protecting product-name and price legibility.
  • Reusable structure: used repeatable layouts so new promotions and labels could be extended without redesigning the system from scratch.

Scope and limits

This case demonstrates design thinking and visual consistency. The mockups are not evidence of increased sales, conversion or a deployed app.

Tools used

Layout systems · Kannada + English typesetting
