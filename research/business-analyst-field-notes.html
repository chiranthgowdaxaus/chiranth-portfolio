Business Analyst Field Notes

A note on how I approach roles that sit between business questions, data, product design, stakeholders, and delivery.

business analysis BI reporting stakeholders product thinking UX communication

01 / Start With The Decision

I try to understand what decision the work is supposed to improve. That keeps analysis from becoming decoration and keeps dashboards from becoming storage rooms for charts.

02 / Find The Mess

The useful problem is usually hidden in messy data, unclear ownership, manual workflows, vague requirements, or interfaces that make people work harder than they need to.

03 / Build The Bridge

  • Translate stakeholder language into questions that can be tested.
  • Turn raw data into summaries, flows, or prototypes that people can react to.
  • Document enough context so the next person can continue the work.

04 / Finish In Human Language

The output should be usable by someone who did not sit inside the analysis process. That might be a KPI summary, a dashboard, a product flow, a handoff doc, or a cleaner visual system.

What This Says About My Work

I am focused on data analyst, business analyst, BI, and operations roles because my strongest work is cross-functional without being scattered. I can read the data, shape the artifact, and explain the tradeoff in plain language.
