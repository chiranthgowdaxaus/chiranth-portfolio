How I workwith data

I check the question and the data before choosing a method.

Define the question

I ask what needs to be decided and who will use the answer. That helps narrow the analysis.

Check the data

I check definitions, missing values and assumptions before choosing a metric or model.

Explain the results

I use charts and a short explanation to show what I found, including anything the data cannot answer.

Record the method

I document the steps and open questions so someone else can rerun the work.
