    The data

    The public UCI Online Retail dataset: every transaction from a UK online gift retailer, 1 December 2010 to 9 December 2011. Historical data, analysed in Python and SQL.

    Cleaning decisions

    • Gross posted value: positive quantities at positive unit prices.
    • Credits: negative quantities, reported separately instead of netted away, so returns stay visible.
    • Duplicates: exact repeated rows removed, on the assumption they were double-posted.
    • Non-merchandise lines: postage (DOT, POST) and manual adjustments (M) are kept in totals but flagged in product rankings.
    • Customer segments exclude rows with no customer ID.

    Limits

    December 2011 ends on the 9th, so compare complete months only. There's no cost or profit data, so this describes revenue, not margin.
