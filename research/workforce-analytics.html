Method & definitionsHow each number is calculated, and what it can't tell you

The data

A fictional organisation of about 1,000 employee records across eight departments, January to October 2026, in Australian dollars. The data is synthetic, so the reporting could be built and tested without anyone's personal data.

What I built

  • SQL pipeline: monthly snapshots of headcount, FTE, hires and exits, joined to plan and salary budget tables.
  • Excel workbook: a reporting pack with month and department filters, variance highlighting and a headcount-against-plan chart.
  • Compensation planning model: salary against budget by department, ready for what-if changes.
  • Power BI: practice files that rebuild the same measures.

Definitions

  • Turnover = monthly exits ÷ average of opening and closing headcount.
  • Salary actual is monthly base salary. It excludes employer on-costs and bonuses.
  • Mean base pay gap = 1 − mean women's FTE annual base pay ÷ mean men's. It's suppressed when either group has fewer than 10 people, so small teams can't be identified.

October 2026: 875 people, 833.0 FTE, 40.9% women, 2.5% mean base pay gap.
